Chapter 1 · General
Article 1
Name

This society shall be called the Korean Intercollegiate Society for Analytic Philosophy (KISAP).

Article 2
Purpose

The society aims to promote the study and discussion of analytic philosophy among university students.

Chapter 2 · Membership
Article 5
Qualification

Membership is open to individuals who share the purpose of the society and are engaged in study or research in related fields.

Article 6
Admission

Applicants shall submit the prescribed form. Admission is confirmed upon approval by the steering committee.

Article 7
Rights & Duties

Members may attend all events and access the archive, and shall pay the annual fee and observe these bylaws.

Article 8
Withdrawal

A member may withdraw at any time by written notice to the secretary.

Chapter 3 · Officers
Article 9
Officers

The society shall have a president, a vice-president, a secretary and a treasurer.

Chapter 4 · Meetings
Article 12
General Meeting

The general meeting shall be held once a year and convened by the president.

Chapter 5 · Finance
Article 15
Revenue

The society's revenue consists of membership fees, donations and other income.

Supplementary
Addendum
Effective Date

These bylaws take effect from the date of adoption.
